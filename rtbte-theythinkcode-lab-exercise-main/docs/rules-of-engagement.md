# Rules of Engagement

## Scope

- Only the workshop systems are in scope
- Do not attack anything outside the lab
- Do not change the target outside the approved exercise steps

## Team behavior

- Share findings inside the team
- Keep notes in the provided format
- Escalate confirmed incidents through the SOC chain

## Evidence

- Capture timestamps
- Save screenshots when useful
- Record alert IDs, source IPs, usernames, and affected hosts

## Safety

- Use the lab only
- Stop if the exercise coordinator pauses the event
